# Current candidate internal audit — R14

**Mainnet is not approved. Noah remains V2; Sushi is unactivated and Curve is deferred.** This is our internal review of the current candidate before any external handoff. Nothing has been sent to an external auditor or published. R14 has 3 open findings, including a creator launch blocker; it is not an unconditional deploy approval.

The review follows the current source, rather than assuming earlier fixes are missing or borrowing an old green build. It combines targeted adversarial code review across eight areas, fresh local tests/builds, exact source/WASM identity checks, current dependency advisories and a separate public wallet availability probe. Coverage and its limits are explicit in the [structured audit](../release/internal-audit.json). A lexical scan, a test total and a read-only wallet probe do not certify absence of defects or prove production readiness.
